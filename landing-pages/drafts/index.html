Landing drafts

Eight hero concepts on common themes. Each plays a short intro and then has one thing to play with. Click to open full size.

01
Pennyfintech, savings app

Spare change from each purchase flies into a glass jar as coins. Switch round-up between €1, €5 and €10 and the jar refills.
02
MarginAI writing tool, SaaS

A bloated product update. Click any sentence and the filler gets struck out and folds away, the word count drops.
03
Second Crackcoffee roastery, shop

The roast curve draws itself while the bean darkens. Drag along the curve to drop earlier or later, and the coffee, notes and bean change.
04
Haldearchitecture studio

An axonometric building drops in floor by floor. Hover a floor and everything above lifts to show its plan.
05
Pacerunning app

Contour lines rise, a half-marathon route draws over them with live stats. Scrub the elevation profile or the map to replay the run.
06
Casa Salboutique hotel

An arched window onto the Atlantic runs from dawn to golden hour. Move across the window to change the hour, the page tints with it.
07
Lowtidemusic festival

The line-up is set in a variable font. Letters near the cursor swell, and with the preview playing they pump to the beat.
08
Dewskincare

A drop falls from the pipette and becomes the cursor, a water lens that magnifies the page. Click to leave drops that slowly dry.
